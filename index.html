A Heartfelt Devotional Offering

Hare Krishna Pamho Prji

Thank you for giving me your association in Gaur Nitai Home. It is literally by your mercy I have a home in your association of devotees. I am very much touched by your spontaneous attraction to dieties, and your resourcefulness in serving devotees by taking challenging services such as Accomodation booking. I especially was impressed by you going into details such as ordering the names of parents and devotees, choosing to use legal name instead of HG SItanath Prasad Prabhu, going a day in advance , taking care of end to end details etc.

I wish to seek forgiveness from you for all my offenses such as not doing diety worship, not cleaning the house and many more. I will try not to repeat these mistakes to please a great Vaishnava like you.

Yas

Gopalkrishna
